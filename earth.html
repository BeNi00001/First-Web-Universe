THE EARTH

Earth is the only place in the universe where we know life exists. It sits at just the right distance from the Sun — close enough to stay warm, far enough not to boil — so water can stay liquid. And there is a lot of it: oceans cover about 71% of the surface. Earth also protects itself. Its atmosphere blocks harmful radiation from space, and a magnetic field created by its hot, spinning core shields us from the solar wind.
# Source of the information for this page: Earth.

  • Earth is about 4.5 billion years old.
  • A day on Earth is actually 23.9 hours long, not a full 24.
  • Our Moon keeps Earth from wobbling too much on its axis, which has helped keep our climate stable for thousands of years.
