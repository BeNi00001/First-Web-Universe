THE SUN

The bright object we see in the sky during the day is a star: the Sun. It is so massive that its gravity bends space and time around it ,this is what Einstein's theory of general relativity describes — and that curvature is what keeps the planets in orbit.

At the same time, the whole solar system travels together around the centre of our galaxy.The Sun is also what keeps Earth warm enough for liquid water and life. Without its energy, our planet would slowly freeze, and nothing we know of could survive here.
# Source of the information for this page: Our Sun: Facts.

  • The Sun is about 4.5 billion years old.
  • It is about 93 million miles (150 million kilometers) from Earth and it is our solar system’s only star.
  • Our Sun is a medium-sized star with a radius of about 435,000 miles (700,000 kilometers).
